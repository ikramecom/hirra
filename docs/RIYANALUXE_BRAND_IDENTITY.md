# RIYANALUXE — Brand Identity

## Name
**RIYANALUXE** (ريانا لوكس) — luxury Maghreb home fragrance. Pronounceable in Arabic and French; signals premium positioning.

## Tagline
- **AR:** فنّ الضيافة والريحة
- **FR:** L'art du foyer parfumé

## Positioning
Premium Moroccan/Maghreb lifestyle brand centered on **luxury home fragrance** (Mabkhara hero) with complementary home rituals (stone bath mat, closet dehumidifier).

## Logo system
| Asset | Path | Usage |
|-------|------|--------|
| Wordmark + monogram | `/public/brand/riyana-logo.svg` | Header, packaging |
| Favicon / app icon | `/public/favicon.svg` | Flame droplet on obsidian |
| Monogram only | `RiyanaluxeLogo` `variant="mark"` | Mobile header |

### Monogram concept
Circular seal with **gold flame** (incense / warmth) on **charcoal** — timeless, not cartoon, not startup.

### Typography
- **Display / EN:** Cormorant Garamond — editorial luxury
- **Body AR:** IBM Plex Arabic — clarity + Arabic luxury
- **Body FR/EN UI:** Inter

## Voice
- Cinematic, warm, hospitable — never aggressive discount language
- Darija-friendly Arabic + elegant French
- COD reassurance via WhatsApp confirmation, not pressure
